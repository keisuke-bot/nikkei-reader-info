# nikkei-reader

A personal tool that organizes newspaper articles saved by its owner. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to anyone else.

In Google Drive it works only with the files that this tool itself created in the owner's Drive (Google OAuth scope: drive.file). It cannot see any other files.

個人用の新聞記事整理ツールです。持ち主本人だけが使い、ほかの人には提供していません。Google ドライブでは、このツール自身が作ったファイルだけを扱います。
